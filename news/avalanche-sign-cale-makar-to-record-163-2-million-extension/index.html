Avalanche sign Cale Makar to record $163.2 million extension

It is the largest contract in NHL history.

DENVER, Colo. (ASW) · By AllStatesWire Staff · August 28, 2026

DENVER, Colo. (ASW) — The Colorado Avalanche signed defenseman Cale Makar to an eight-year, $163.2 million contract extension, the largest in National Hockey League history.

Makar is the first player to earn more than $20 million a year in the league.
